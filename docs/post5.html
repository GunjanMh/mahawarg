Without Faith

A parrot remains a caged parrot if it does not have faith in what it's saying. Simply reiterating someone else's words does not mean you mean them or even believe them—much less that you truly understand them. At times, we hear something, a wise person quotes an aphorism, a heated argument concludes in a singular statement, or even when a loved one issues a confession. We simply hear it. But don't believe in it. Don't have faith in it. Is it a part of our thinking? No. Should it be a part of our memory then? Well, technically no. But more often than not, it does become.

Any thought, desire or even a dream that lacks faith is merely a borrowed statement, just an arrangement of certain words in a chosen language that does have meaning but not worth. Not for you. the one who doesn't have faith in it. And even the simplest, most benign ideas, that aren't worth a dime to many might become the greatest catalysts of inordinate transformations. Not just kind, even the most viscious of thoughts transmute into physical reality when acted upon with immense faith. How else would a tyrant rule with sincere cruelty over the world? a tiny, negligible fraction of the human population create havoc for the greatest nations of the world? a man stab another in the heart with a grin on his face?

Faith wouldn't know how to discriminate between good and bad, kind and evil. It would only propel it's adherent to achieve the practical reality of thoughts conceived. I wonder if we, who consider ourselves the pinnacle of human evolution, realize that nothing is achievable without faith—and more importantly, that anything is within reach when it is infused with it. What is the point, really, of doing something—of spending our time and effort on something we don't even believe in? Something we know isn't our true calling. Who taught us this compromise? Who plotted for us to live in a way that we do not even realize we are, in fact, living? Did any one person come up with this scheme, or are all of us—the most evolved humans to have existed—to be blamed for plotting a unanimous scheme? A scheme that aims at pushing us into an abyss where neither faith nor life respires.

I lack faith in so many things I do. Contemplating upon those activites and why I engage in them perplexes me more than high school trignometry ever did. I had help there. A brilliant and super kind math teacher. But here, I'm on my own. And so are you, I suppose. It's okay to take time and figure out but it's not okat to live in denial. If you can lie to yourself, you're not just a liar you're also a cheat. Cheating yourself out of a fair chance to live a life that, if cosmos and science are to completely trusted upon, has emerged after immense chaos, trials, sacrifies and intelligence.
