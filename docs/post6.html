Need to not be Stuck

It's true that past is cemented and should not be cried over. But sometimes when the past reminds you of a stronger, better version of you you wish that if it returns - just one more time! Simply to give you the courage to be that amazing, daring, fearless self again- you'll morph into that version and never let it go. It's a mistake to have let it go and there's no way you'd commit that mistake again. But, you need that past - one more time. It needs to instill that old self in you. Between what we were and what we want to be is where all of us struggle. Some struggle to try and get back the courage that though were not the most rational choices, made us feel more alive than ever. While some wish to emabrk on this road that they have long stared down at, dreamt of in their wildest dreams and even mapped it all out before laying a toe onto its gravel. It's the one that can change their life, atleast is a sincere attempt to do so, but something lacks or restrains this desire from becoming an action.

Is it important that one needs to lose oneself so that the better past or the brgith future can entice the mind to revolutionise the present state of being? Is that how everyone who's found a solid footing in life did so? When I admire the past, I admire the decisions I took, the confidence I placed in myself and how I never let anyone run all over me. I respected that, no matter the noise around me. Fast forward 6 years, and I find myself in a different place. It's a weird environment where I do exactly the opposite of my previous being. I am neither happy with that nor do I have high regards for this way of being. But still, I end up delaying all my decisions, thinking it's important to be rational and practical and feasible and what not. I shouldn't only think about what I want but what people around me expect from me, what do they need me to do, what would not hurt them? It's annoying to have been morphed into this person. But some part, some twig within has refused to change. Because it twitches over and over again, to remind me that I am not what I want to be. I am not even working to be what I want to be. And yes, it can be that person's fault or the timing issue or simply fate's misdoings, but while all these might have contributed to the present, I , for sure, am the reason behind it. Life is a cause and effect game. If nothing is significant enough to "cause", it simply won't be powerful enough to "effect". And changing your fundamentals is more than enough to bring about order of magnitude changes in your life.

It's either too judicous or too fool hardy a decision. ALways. Whenever I think I should take a step, however small, but in the direction where I can find my previous self- the one I want to love and live for, the decision I can take appears to be on either of these extremes. And while I think it's okay to look stupid sometimes, I am not able to deny why the sensibility should be simply ignored when it's right there in front of me. And that's how everytime, I land back to square one! There is no decision, no movement. Just because I cannot admit that I am choosing- with all my presence of mind- the opposite of being practical and rational and feasible, I end up not choosing at all. And the need to not be stuck, proliferates like moss on a rotting log.

As I'm putting it down in words, I believe there's no way forward ( or even backward) unless I commit to the extreme. It's okay to not be making the visibly more rational choice sometimes. Even if it's not okay, maybe it's okay to figure that out post decisiom-making. Else, how does one escape this quiescent being? Only the dead fish flow with water.
